# AnyAirline Connector

**AI passenger cabin & IFE experience for X-Plane 12, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.**

AnyAirline brings the missing passenger-cabin layer into your simulator.

Instead of flying with a silent aircraft behind the cockpit door, AnyAirline adds cabin-style announcements, boarding and deboarding ambience, safety and service media, workshop assets, AI-generated crew voices and a live passenger IFE map.

It is not just a cabin announcement player.

It is a system designed to make each flight feel like it belongs to a specific airline, route and purpose.

---

## What is AnyAirline?

AnyAirline is a cabin immersion platform for flight simulator users.

The idea is simple:

> Your cockpit is alive.  
> Your cabin should be too.

Modern flight simulation already offers highly detailed aircraft, real-world procedures, live weather, realistic navigation and virtual airline operations. But in many flights, the passenger side of the experience is still missing.

AnyAirline focuses on that missing layer:

- cabin and gate-style announcements
- AI-generated crew voices
- boarding and deboarding ambience
- safety and service media
- passenger IFE map
- workshop content
- route and airline-specific cabin flow
- support for virtual airline operations
- SimBrief or manual flight setup

The goal is not to replace VATSIM, IVAO, ACARS, virtual airlines or existing aircraft systems.

The goal is to make the flight feel more alive from the passenger perspective.

---

## What can it be used for?

AnyAirline is designed for many different types of flights and cabin experiences.

For example:

- an ultra low-cost Ryanair-style sector with scratchcard announcements, onboard sales and unmistakable low-cost cabin energy
- an ultra long-haul flight with multiple inflight services, cruise updates, meal rounds and proper arrival preparation
- a Maldivian Island Hopper-style experience with short tropical sectors and a very different cabin mood
- a Kathmandu to Lukla local mountain flight where the cabin feels small, local and very different from a typical airline service
- a holiday charter flight with Czech and English announcements, boarding music and vacation atmosphere from the moment passengers step onboard
- a virtual airline flight with its own branded passenger experience, custom announcements and shareable IFE map
- a cargo sector with a crew-focused atmosphere instead of pretending there are passengers onboard
- a pilgrimage or special-purpose flight with route-specific announcements and cabin flow
- a premium international flight where the cabin experience changes throughout the journey, not just at boarding and landing

AnyAirline is not about playing one generic sound file.

It is about making the aircraft behind the cockpit feel alive.

## Main features

### AI cabin announcements

Generate airline-style announcements using AI voices.

Depending on your setup, AnyAirline can support different crew roles, such as:

- captain
- gate agent
- cabin crew

Paid AI credits unlock cloud-generated voices, multilingual cabin flows, custom airline-style announcements and more advanced templates.

---

### Local/offline TTS

The free tier includes local/offline English TTS.

This allows you to try the basic announcement workflow without relying on paid AI voice generation.

---

### Passenger IFE map

AnyAirline includes a passenger-facing IFE map.

The IFE map can show information such as:

- route
- aircraft position
- estimated time of arrival
- destination context
- passenger-facing flight information

This can also be useful for virtual airlines, streamers or anyone who wants to share the passenger side of the flight with someone outside the simulator.

---

### Workshop assets

AnyAirline supports workshop-style content.

The workshop is intended for passenger-facing ambience and media, such as:

- boarding music
- deboarding music
- airport ambience
- cabin ambience
- safety and service media
- airline-style passenger experience assets

The idea is to let the community help build richer cabin experiences for different routes, regions and airline styles.

---

### SimBrief and manual flight setup

AnyAirline can be used with SimBrief OFP import or manual flight setup.

This allows you to create the cabin flow around the flight you are actually operating.

---

### Virtual airline support

AnyAirline can work alongside virtual airline systems.

It does not replace ACARS, dispatch, schedules or VA tracking. Instead, it can add a branded passenger-cabin layer on top of existing virtual airline operations.

Possible use cases:

- custom VA announcements
- branded boarding atmosphere
- shared passenger IFE map
- route-specific cabin flows
- special events or group flights

## Installation

### 1. Download the connector

Download the latest AnyAirline Connector release from the official source.

### 2. Install or extract the connector

Follow the installation instructions for your operating system.

Depending on the release format, this may be a simple installer or a portable application.

### 3. Create a free AnyAirline account

Go to:

https://anyairline.app/

Create a free account or sign in with your existing account.

### 4. Start the connector

Open the AnyAirline Connector before starting or during your simulator session.

### 5. Connect your simulator

Start your simulator and load your flight.

The connector will act as the bridge between your simulator and AnyAirline.

### 6. Create or import your flight

You can either:

- import a SimBrief OFP
- or create the flight manually

### 7. Choose your cabin setup

Select the relevant cabin options, such as:

- airline or airline style
- route
- language
- crew roles
- voices
- cabin flow
- workshop assets

### 8. Start your flight

Once everything is connected, AnyAirline can follow your flight and trigger the cabin experience.

## Basic workflow

A typical AnyAirline workflow looks like this:

1. Plan your flight
2. Open AnyAirline
3. Import SimBrief or enter flight details manually
4. Choose languages and crew voices
5. Connect the desktop connector
6. Start boarding
7. Fly normally
8. Let the cabin experience follow the flight
9. Use or share the passenger IFE map
10. Provide feedback if something does not feel right
